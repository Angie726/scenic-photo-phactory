About Scenic Photo Phactory

We Capture, Create & Print Your Memories

Our Story

Scenic Photo Phactory is a professional photography, printing and branding studio serving individuals, families, organisations and businesses across Kenya.

We combine creative photography, careful editing and high-quality printing to deliver products that preserve memories and promote brands.

Our goal is to provide dependable, affordable and professional services—from capturing the photograph to producing the final printed product.

Whether you need a passport photo, a wedding album, branded merchandise for your business, or anything in between, we bring creativity and quality together in one place.
